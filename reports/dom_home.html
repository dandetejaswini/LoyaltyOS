Append-Only Ledger Journal

All point credits and debits are recorded as immutable financial journal entries. Zero in-place mutations or silent historical deletions.

FIFO Lot Tracking & Idempotent Expiry

Points lots are provisioned with 365-day expiry dates. Redemptions consume lots in strict FIFO order; batch expiration runs idempotently.

Concurrency-Safe Atomic Redemptions

Pessimistic row locking on loyalty accounts prevents double-spending and race conditions under simultaneous traffic.

Proportional Return Reversals

Order returns reverse earned points strictly proportional to eligible net spend, maintaining foreign-key links to original accruals.
